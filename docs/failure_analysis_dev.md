# Failure analysis on `peru-dev` (phase C, tasks 6.1–6.5)

Date: 2026-10-14. Split: `peru-dev` only (174 chips, 112 with water). No test split was
used. All numbers come from `outputs/analysis_dev/{strata,calibration,chips}.csv`,
produced by `scripts/analyze_dev.py` from the per-chip probabilities saved by
`floodseg.callbacks.SaveValPredictions`; tables are generated by
`scripts/report_dev.py` (full output: `outputs/analysis_dev/tables.md`). Runs and
commits are listed in `docs/EXPERIMENT_LOG.md`.

Models: Peru-only (3 seeds each, RunPod) and Sen1Floods11-only (seed 0, Mac); the
Sen1Floods11 → Peru fine-tuning row is added when its runs finish. Values are
mean ± std over seeds; a single number means one seed. Prediction = probability > 0.5.
Only labelled pixels count (label ≠ −1).

Check: water IoU recomputed from the saved probabilities equals the logged
`val/IoU_Flood` of every run to 4 decimals.

## Finding 1: most errors are at water edges, and the foundation models are worse there

| Distance to the label's water edge (water IoU) | Peru U-Net | Peru TerraMind | Peru Prithvi |
|---|---|---|---|
| edge, ≤ 1 px (4.4 % of pixels) | 0.761 ± 0.007 | 0.695 ± 0.007 | 0.661 ± 0.008 |
| 2–3 px | 0.930 ± 0.000 | 0.931 ± 0.002 | 0.925 ± 0.002 |
| 4–10 px | 0.961 ± 0.001 | 0.959 ± 0.001 | 0.960 ± 0.001 |
| > 10 px | 0.985 ± 0.001 | 0.979 ± 0.002 | 0.981 ± 0.001 |

Share of all errors (FP + FN) on the ≤ 1 px edge band: U-Net 59.4 ± 0.5 %, TerraMind
63.3 ± 0.5 %, Prithvi 66.9 ± 0.8 %; within 3 px: 69.9 %, 71.5 %, 75.2 %.
Away from edges the three models are within 0.6 IoU points; the U-Net's overall lead
comes mostly from the edge band. Small water bodies point the same way:

| Water-body size (recall %) | Peru U-Net | Peru TerraMind | Peru Prithvi |
|---|---|---|---|
| < 1 ha (37,799 px) | 57.96 ± 4.19 | 46.01 ± 2.96 | 44.89 ± 1.57 |
| 1–10 ha | 75.39 ± 1.23 | 73.91 ± 1.10 | 72.76 ± 0.95 |
| 10–100 ha | 86.26 ± 0.74 | 85.45 ± 0.88 | 84.73 ± 0.95 |
| > 100 ha | 98.17 ± 0.14 | 97.52 ± 0.13 | 97.50 ± 0.20 |

Likely cause (hypothesis, not tested): both foundation models see the image as 16 × 16
pixel patches, so fine detail must be recovered by the decoder, while the U-Net keeps
full-resolution skip connections. Part of the edge error is also label imprecision:
the labels are rasterized UNOSAT polygons.

## Finding 2: training on Peru makes models miss low-contrast water

Recall on true water, split by the pixel's NDWI (B03 − B08)/(B03 + B08):

| Water NDWI (recall %) | Peru U-Net | Peru TerraMind | Peru Prithvi | S1F11-only U-Net | S1F11-only TerraMind | S1F11-only Prithvi |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| < −0.1 (36,034 px) | 65.20 ± 2.96 | 61.11 ± 1.60 | 59.88 ± 1.13 | 35.05 | 36.61 | 37.61 |
| −0.1 to 0 (85,409 px) | 57.58 ± 0.77 | 54.77 ± 2.08 | 53.57 ± 0.46 | 71.41 | 78.33 | 81.22 |
| 0 to 0.1 (171,942 px) | 60.79 ± 2.18 | 58.74 ± 1.01 | 58.70 ± 1.65 | 81.99 | 80.62 | 80.73 |
| 0.1 to 0.3 (893,962 px) | 95.01 ± 0.16 | 91.93 ± 0.68 | 92.27 ± 0.84 | 96.57 | 96.33 | 96.57 |
| > 0.3 (3,700,291 px) | 99.88 ± 0.02 | 99.82 ± 0.03 | 99.65 ± 0.08 | 99.76 | 99.91 | 99.94 |

On water with NDWI between −0.1 and 0.1 (257,351 px, 5 % of dev water) the Peru-only
models find 54–61 %, the Sen1Floods11-only models 71–82 %. Missed water with NDWI < 0.1
is 32–40 % of all Peru-only errors (this overlaps with the edge band). In the images
these are cyan, turbid or shallow channels in the SWIR false colour (chips
`2023-03-22_E522240_N9354240`, `2023-03-22_E527360_N9354240`,
`2023-03-22_E532480_N9318400`). Sen1Floods11-only models, in turn, call far more desert
water (false-positive rate on WorldCover "bare/sparse": 1.7–3.2 % vs 0.3–0.5 %).
Possible causes: similar surfaces labelled dry in `peru-train` (label inconsistency), or
the low water share in `peru-train` (4.6 % of labelled pixels vs 12.4 % in dev).

## Finding 3: a share of the "errors" are label errors

The 10 chips with the most Peru-only errors hold 32 % of all errors. Looking at them
(figures: `outputs/analysis_dev/figs/`, `scripts/plot_dev_chips.py`):

| Chip | What the image shows |
|---|---|
| 2023-04-01_E517120_N9349120 | clear dark water labelled dry; all models call it water (label omission) |
| 2023-04-01_E522240_N9349120 | as above at the west end of a lake, plus edge misses |
| 2023-04-01_E527360_N9303040 | speckled label (pixel holes) inside a flooded area; models fill it |
| 2023-03-22_E522240_N9354240, _E527360_N9354240, _E532480_N9318400 | cyan turbid/shallow water labelled water; Peru-only models miss it (Finding 2) |
| 2023-03-22_E512000_N9354240 | flooded vegetation labelled water; partly missed |
| 2023-04-01_E527360_N9313280, 2023-04-01_E512000_N9354240 | water in cloud shadow / next to cloud |
| 2023-03-22_E532480_N9349120 | edge misses along a large lake |

WorldCover agrees: on dry-labelled pixels that WorldCover maps as permanent water
(85,896 px) the Peru-only models predict water on 31.7–36.9 % (vs ≤ 2.8 % on any other
class, the highest being herbaceous wetland). The dev score is therefore a lower bound and is limited by label quality; the
thesis must say so.

## Finding 4: clouds matter less than expected

Pixels within 20 px of thick cloud are 8.4 % of dev pixels and carry 7.8–9.6 % of the
Peru-only errors; IoU falls to 0.66–0.68 at 5–20 px from cloud (vs 0.93–0.95 beyond
50 px), but there is little water there. Date matters little (U-Net 0.945 on 22 March
vs 0.937 on 1 April).

## Calibration

All Peru-only models are well calibrated on dev (ECE < 1 %); the uncertainty flags
errors only moderately (AUROC 0.71), as expected when most errors are thin edge bands
and label errors. Temperature scaling and ensembles (section 5) are still to do.

## Decision record (task 6.5): candidate methods for phase D

Dominant failure modes: (1) water edges and small water bodies, worst for the
patch-based foundation models; (2) low-contrast (turbid/shallow) water missed after
Peru-only training; (3) label noise in UNOSAT.

1. **Full-resolution detail for the foundation models**: add a light convolutional
   branch / skip connections from the input image to the decoder (hybrid FM + CNN),
   or a boundary-aware loss. Target: edge-band IoU and < 1 ha recall.
2. **Keep the low-contrast-water skill from broader training**: Sen1Floods11 → Peru
   fine-tuning (running now) or mixed training; check whether it keeps Peru-only edges
   while recovering NDWI < 0.1 recall.
3. **Noise-robust training on UNOSAT labels**: ignore a 1-px band at label edges in the
   loss, or a noise-robust loss; report a boundary-tolerant metric next to IoU.
4. **Sentinel-1 for TerraMind** (needs the advisor's OK): radar sees through cloud and
   separates water from wet soil; useful for the cloud-adjacent and turbid cases.

To decide after the Sen1Floods11 → Peru results: which of 1–3 to pursue first.
