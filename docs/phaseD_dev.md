Status 2026-10-15. Sources: `outputs/phaseD/dev_table.md` (all rows, generated by
`scripts/phaseD_dev_table.py`), `outputs/phaseD/dev_table_runs.csv` (per run),
`outputs/phaseD/paired_deltas.csv`, `outputs/phaseD/oracle_selection.md`
(`scripts/phaseD_oracle.py`), `outputs/phaseD/water_frac_trajectory.csv`. Method details and

## Question

Can a model trained on Sen1Floods11 be adapted to Peru using only **unlabelled** Peru
Sentinel-2 images (the 753-image `peru-train` pool), so that UNOSAT labels are needed only for
selection and evaluation? The reference points are the source-only model (lower bound) and the
same backbone trained on Peru labels (upper bound).

## Findings

1. **Simple statistics alignment works best, and which alignment works depends on the
   backbone.** The BatchNorm U-Net gains on every seed from AdaBN (re-estimating BN statistics on
   the pool). TerraMind's encoder has no BN, and decoder-only AdaBN hurts it. Test-time
   histogram matching of the input bands helps TerraMind on every seed and removes its seed
   sensitivity (std 0.091 → 0.006). Neither needs training or Peru labels.
2. **FDA fails completely.** Swapping the low-frequency amplitude per band shifts each band's
   mean independently and so changes B03 − B11, the water signal itself.
3. **Self-training, as pre-registered, is harmful or seed-dependent on both backbones.** Most of
   the TerraMind drop comes from the pre-registered evaluation step (AdaBN after
   self-training), not from self-training itself. Without it (post-hoc), `st` is stable
   (0.758 ± 0.006) and slightly above source-only, but still below histogram matching on every
   seed. For U-Net, `st` without AdaBN is still below plain AdaBN on every seed. Without AdaBN, the masked
   variant (`mic`) is worse than `st` on both backbones; with AdaBN the two are equal for U-Net,
   and both collapse for TerraMind.
4. **Oracle selection does not rescue self-training.** With dev labels choosing the
   configuration and checkpoint, U-Net reaches 0.914, which is the default seed-0 run and only
   +0.009 over AdaBN on that seed. The TerraMind grid with AdaBN is uninformative because the
   AdaBN pass depresses every cell. Without AdaBN (post-hoc, final checkpoints), the best
   TerraMind cell is 0.796 (α 0.999, final q 20 %). That is still below HM on the same seed
   (0.815), and fewer pseudo-labels are better throughout. The dev-tuned MNDWI threshold
   (0.925), which uses the same labels, beats all of them.
5. **The source models' Peru error is false positives** (recall ≈ 0.98, precision 0.71–0.85),
   on bright desert and coastal surfaces (`docs/failure_analysis_dev.md`). Alignment methods
   raise precision: AdaBN takes U-Net from 0.848 to 0.919, HM takes TerraMind from 0.714 to 0.853.

## Where the best methods still fail, and calibration (tasks 5.2, 5.3)

Source: `outputs/phaseD/analysis/` (`scripts/phaseD_analysis.py`); full numbers and definitions
in METHODS_NOTES 2026-10-15 "Where the best label-free methods still fail".

- **What alignment fixes:** false positives on bare desert, which holds 33.5 M of the 40 M
  labelled pixels. FPR on bare/sparse land falls from 0.026 to 0.012 for U-Net + AdaBN and
  from 0.063 to 0.024 for TerraMind + HM (Peru-trained: 0.003 and 0.004).
- **What it does not fix:** the label edge (≤ 1 px IoU: U-Net 0.611, TerraMind 0.580, vs 0.740
  and 0.667 Peru-trained), small water bodies (< 1 ha) and low-contrast water (NDWI < 0.1).
- **HM trades one error for another (TerraMind):** faint water is pushed to dry (recall 0.02–0.32
  for NDWI < 0.1), and mid-NDWI dry land is pushed to water (FPR 0.51 for NDWI 0.1–0.3, vs 0.18
  for source-only).
- **Calibration without Peru labels is possible:** with a temperature fitted on Sen1Floods11
  `valid` plus the 3-seed ensemble, near-water ECE is U-Net 5.33 % and TerraMind 6.62 %. The
  Peru-trained single models reach 5.42 % and 6.17 %.
- **The remaining errors are confident:** TerraMind + HM error AUROC over all pixels is 0.52,
  near chance, so its uncertainty cannot flag its mistakes.

## Caveats

- Dev results only. The test splits will be evaluated once, after the methods are frozen.
- The no-AdaBN rows were chosen after seeing dev, so they are diagnostic and not headline. The
  pre-registered BN rule was not revisited after the decoder-AdaBN result came in, before the
  pod runs. METHODS_NOTES records this as a missed warning.
- The collapse watch was one-sided: it detects only a shrinking water area. "0 collapse
  events" is not evidence of stability.
- The oracle grid is seed 0 only. Its intermediate checkpoints (1,000–3,000) exist only as
  AdaBN-evaluated predictions because the weights were deleted on the pod. Only the final
  checkpoints were re-evaluated without AdaBN (`outputs/phaseD/oracle_grid_noadabn.md`).
- Gap closed on means is distorted by TerraMind's weak seed 1. Use the paired deltas.
- Prithvi has no source models yet (task 5.4).
